# Narrative Enhancement

Narrative enhancement is optional. Activate it only when the user explicitly asks to invent, write, or attach a story/backstory, or explicitly requests `叙事增强` as an output mode. Requests for symbolism, meaning, mood, emotional tension, or a more narrative-looking image affect the visual proposition but do not authorize story text.

## Mode selection

- **Pure visual transformation:** default; produce only the artwork and a brief concept note.
- **Inferred narrative:** invent a fictional story from visible relationships in the source.
- **User-specified narrative:** treat the user's story, event, character relation, and emotional outcome as content constraints.

Do not ask which mode the user wants when their wording already makes it clear.

## Build the story before generating

Create an internal one-sentence narrative beat that answers:

1. What has just happened or is about to happen?
2. What visible object, gesture, path, light, or distance carries the change?
3. What emotional contradiction gives the image tension?

Use that beat to choose the spatial metaphor. The carrier must still be a visible or directly evidenced source form. In inferred narrative mode, do not add a new factual object or event; reinterpret an existing gesture, distance, object, boundary, light, or void. In user-specified narrative mode, one story element absent from the source may appear only as an abstract symbol, negative shape, color event, or distant suggestion, while the source anchor remains factual authority.

Do not invent sensitive real-world claims about identifiable people. When the source is a personal or documentary photo and the user provides no facts, frame the story as fictional or symbolic.

## Story writing after generation

After the finished image, provide:

- an optional short title when it adds value;
- a restrained fictional story that follows the user's language: roughly 120–220 Chinese characters for Chinese, or about 80–140 words for alphabetic languages, unless the user requests another length;
- language that extends the image rather than explaining every symbol.

Avoid generic epic narration, motivational slogans, plot summaries, and repeated phrases such as “命运的齿轮”. Keep the story specific to the visible scene and the chosen contradiction.

Do not place the title or story inside the image unless the user explicitly requests visible typography.
